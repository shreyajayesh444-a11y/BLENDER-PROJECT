# Focuso: A Sensory-Aware Focus Pod (Blender 3D Product Design)

Focuso is a **3D product design concept** modelled and rendered in **Blender**. It is a discreet, tactile companion for people who experience sudden distress, sensory overload, or restlessness in everyday places like classrooms, offices, and public transport.

The product has two parts: a pair of soft **sound pods** and a **textured, stress-ball-like charging shell** that gives calming vibration. This project focuses on the **form, ergonomics, materials, and visual design** of the device.

![Focuso pods](images/annotated/pods-touch-pad.jpg)

## Design brief

Many support tools for anxiety, ADHD, and panic are medical-looking, socially visible, or emotionally cold, which makes people hesitant to use them in public. The goal was to design something that:

- looks like an everyday personal accessory rather than a medical device
- can be used **quietly**, without drawing attention
- offers comfort through **touch, vibration, and sound**
- keeps the person in control instead of making them ask for help

## Design concept

### 1. The pods
Two compact, glossy black pods shaped to sit comfortably in the ear.
- A **touch pad** on the outer surface to pause or change the calming sound
- Smooth, rounded forms with no sharp edges, for comfort during longer wear

![Pods, side view](images/renders/pods-side-view.png)
![Pods, front view](images/renders/pods-front-view.png)

### 2. The textured shell
A rounded case that holds the pods and doubles as a fidget and grounding object.
- **Soft, stress-ball texture** that invites repetitive touch
- A **switch to turn vibration on** for calming pulses
- A **plug point with indicator light** for charging

## Companion app concept

The physical device is designed to work alongside a simple app concept:

- A library of **calming and focus sounds** (nature loops, white noise, binaural beats), with the option to add your own
- A **panic alert button** that sends the user's live location to pre-selected trusted contacts
- A **trigger history log** so users can notice patterns in the situations that affect them

### User flow

```
User feels distressed
        |
Uses the Focuso shell for comfort (touch texture + vibration)
        |
Optionally opens the app for calming sound
        |                          |
   Feels better            Still unwell or unsafe
        |                          |
   Session ends         Presses alert button in the app
                                   |
                  Live location + alert sent to trusted contacts
```
